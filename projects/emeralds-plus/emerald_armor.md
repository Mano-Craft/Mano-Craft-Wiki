# 🪖 Emerald Armor

The complete Emerald Armor set provides protection between Diamond and Netherite.

### Obtain
- 🏘️ Found in Weaponsmith village chests
- 🎲 **1% chance** to generate for the boots
- 🎲 **2% chance** to generate for the rest of the armor
