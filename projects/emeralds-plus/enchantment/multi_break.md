If you have Multibreak Enchanted on a tool it lets you mine multiple blocks around the block you mine . The Enchantment goes up to level 5 and a max of a 3 by 3 by 2 mining-area

#### 🔼 Different Levels

- Level 1 : 
  - the block direct **under the block you mine** will break
- Level 2 : 
  - the area you can mine is **2 by 2 by 1** \
⛏️⬛\
⬛⬛
- Level 3 : 
  - the area you can mine is **3 by 3 by 1** \
⬛⬛⬛\
⬛⛏️⬛\
⬛⬛⬛
- Level 4 : 
  - the area you can mine is **2 by 2 by 2** \
⛏️🔳\
🔳🔳
- Level 5 : 
  - the area you can mine is **3 by 3 by 2** \
🔳⛏️🔳\
🔳🔳🔳\
🔳🔳🔳

#### ✅️ Compatibility 

- Emerald Pickaxe
- Emerald Hoe
- Emerald Axe
- Emerald Shovel

#### 💼 Obtain

- Can be enchanted on a book with an enchanting table

#### ❌️ Incompatibility

- Eficiency
