Village enemies does less damage if enchanted

#### 🔼 Different Levels

- Level 1 : 
  - you get **1.5 hearts** less damage
- Level 2 : 
  - you get **2.5 hearts** less damage 
- Level 3 : 
  - you get **3.5 hearts** less damage 

#### ✅️ Compatibility 

- Full Emerald Armor

#### 💼 Obtain

- Can be enchanted on a book with an enchanting table
