## 💰 Trade Luck

If you mine a Workingstation which is connected with a Villager with a tool enchanted with **Trade Luck** 1 , 2 or 3.Replace the workingstation and wait till a Villager gets the job.Then open the Villagers Trade GUI.There is a 5 % Chance per enchanted armor piece of getting **Hero of the Village** .The effect level is determined by the enchantment level used to mine the workstation.The effect time us determined by the Armor Level ( Every Piece can have its own level and the time of the 4 Pieces is added before you get the effect )

#### 🔼 Different Levels on Tool

- Level 1 : 
  - if you get **Hero of the Village**  , you get **Hero of the Village** 1
- Level 2 : 
  - if you get **Hero of the Village**  , you get **Hero of the Village** 2
- Level 3 : 
  - if you get **Hero of the Village**  , you get **Hero of the Village** 3

#### 🔼 Different Levels on Armor Piece

- Level 1 : 
  - the time you get per armor piece is **1 to 15 seconds**
- Level 2 : 
  - the time you get per armor piece is **15 to 30 seconds**
- Level 3 : 
  - the time you get per armor piece is **30 to 45 seconds**

#### ‼️ Only works if

- Enchanted on:
  - The tool your mining the workingstation
  - At least on one Armor Piece

#### ✅️ Compatibility 

- Full Emerald Armor
- Emerald Axe
- Emerald Pickaxe

#### 💼 Obtain

- Can be enchanted on a book with an enchanting table

#### ❌️ Incompatibility

- Multi Break

## 🌾 Fast Farm

If you mine a fully grown crop it automaticly replants and there is a 20% to 35% Chance for a bone to drop

#### 🔼 Different Levels

- Level 1 : 
  - the chance to drop a bone is **20%**
- Level 2 : 
  - the chance to drop a bone is **25%**
- Level 3 : 
  - the chance to drop a bone is **35%**

#### ‼️ Only works if

- The crop is fully grown

#### ✅️ Compatibility 

- Emerald Hoe

#### 💼 Obtain

- Can be enchanted on a book with an enchanting table

#### ❌️ Incompatibility

- Multi Break

## 🧱 Golem Protection
An iron golem does 20% less damage per enchanted armor piece so if you have your full armor enchanted with Golem Protection you are 80% protected of the Irom Golems damage.

#### ✅️ Compatibility 

- Copper Armor
- Iron Armor
- Diamond Armor
- Emerald Armor
- Netherite Armor

#### 💼 Obtain

- Can be enchanted on a book with an enchanting table
