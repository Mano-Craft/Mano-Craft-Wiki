Make more damage to village enemies.And no damage to protected villagers

#### 🔼 Different Levels

- Level 1 : 
  - does **1 heart** more damage to village enemies
- Level 2 : 
  - does **1.75 hearts** more damage to village enemies
- Level 3 : 
  - does **2.5 hearts** more damage to village enemies
- Level 4 : 
  - does **3.25 hearts** more damage to village enemies

#### ✅️ Compatibility 

- Emerald Axe
- Emerald Sword

#### 💼 Obtain

- Can be enchanted on a book with an enchanting table
- Can be enchanted on the suported items in the enchanting table
- Can be traded with a librarian
- Can be found in Chests (e.g. in an strongholds library chest)
