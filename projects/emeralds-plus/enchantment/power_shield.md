No matter when you shield, you won't take any fall damage.You just have to shield when you touch the ground.

#### ✅️ Compatibility 

- Emerald Shield

#### 💼 Obtain

- Can be enchanted on a book with an enchanting table
- Can be enchanted on the suported items in the enchanting table
- Can be traded with a librarian
- Can be found in Chests (e.g. in an strongholds library chest)
