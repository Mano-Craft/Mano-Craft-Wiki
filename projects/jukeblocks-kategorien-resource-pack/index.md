# 🎙️ JukeBlocks Fan Texture Pack

> **Note:** This is an **unofficial fan project** and is not affiliated with EinfachGustaf or the JukeBlocks podcast.

This Resource Pack brings the **JukeBlocks podcast** into Minecraft! Selected music discs have been replaced with custom sounds and matching textures, allowing you to listen to the different JukeBlocks categories directly through a Jukebox.

## Features

* 🎵 Custom sounds for the JukeBlocks categories
* 💿 Custom Music Disc textures
* 🎨 A simple design inspired by the podcast
* 🧱 Easy to use as a Resource Pack
